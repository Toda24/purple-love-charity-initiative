Defending the Fatherless


The Purpose:
Scripture commands us to look after orphans and widows in their distress (James 1:27). Our Orphanage Support program is designed to provide a safe haven, nutrition, and emotional support to children who have lost their parents and widows who have no one to lean on.


The Structure:
We partner with verified local orphanages and identify widows in verified communities. Your donations go towards:

  • Monthly food supplies (Rice, Oil, Protein).
  • Renovating dilapidated living structures.
  • Providing clothing and basic medical checkups.
  • Empowerment programs for widows to start small businesses.
