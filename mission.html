What We Do

The Purple Love Charity Foundation acts as a vessel of God's provision. We verify genuine needs—be it a family unable to pay rent, a student needing tuition, or a rural church needing a roof—and we connect them with funds raised from our partners.


Our Core Values

  • Transparency in Funds
  • Compassion for All
  • Integrity in Service
