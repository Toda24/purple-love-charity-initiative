Advancing the Kingdom


The Purpose:
The gospel must be preached to the ends of the earth. However, many rural pastors and small ministries struggle with the lack of resources to gather effectively. We aim to support these vessels of God so they can focus on the spiritual assignment without financial distraction.


The Structure:
Our ministry support is directed towards strict kingdom advancement projects:

  • Infrastructure: Buying roofing sheets, cement, and chairs for rural churches.
  • Equipment: Providing microphones, speakers, and generators for crusades.
  • Missionary Welfare: Monthly support for missionaries laboring in difficult terrains.
  • Bible Distribution: Providing free bibles to new converts.
